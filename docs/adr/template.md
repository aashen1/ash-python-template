# ADR Template

Use this template when proposing a new architecture decision.

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by [ADR-XXXX](XXXX-title.md)
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: list of people involved in the decision

## Context and Problem Statement

Describe the forces at play, including the technological, business, political,
social, and project local concerns. What is the issue we are seeing that is
motivating this decision or change?

## Considered Options

List the alternatives that were considered, with a short summary of pros and
cons for each.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option, with a justification of why it was selected over the others.

### Positive Consequences

What becomes easier as a result of this change?

### Negative Consequences

What becomes harder or riskier as a result of this change?
